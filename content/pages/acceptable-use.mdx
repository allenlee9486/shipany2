## Introduction

AI Rumpelstiltskin is an independent creative project inspired by a viral AI video meme. This Acceptable Use Policy explains what you may and may not do on this website, including any future upload, prompt or generation features.

## Use the Service Lawfully

You agree to use AI Rumpelstiltskin only for lawful purposes and in compliance with this policy, our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

## Prohibited Content

When the workspace opens for uploads and generation, you may NOT submit, request or distribute content that:

1. **Impersonation** — depicts real, identifiable people in misleading, defamatory or humiliating ways, or fakes their words or actions.
2. **Minors** — involves minors in any inappropriate context, including suggestive or violent scenarios.
3. **Copyright & characters** — infringes copyrights or tries to reproduce protected characters, brands or franchises you do not have rights to.
4. **Sexual content** — contains sexually explicit or pornographic material.
5. **Violence & extremism** — contains graphic violence, gore, or promotes extremist or terrorist ideology.
6. **Hate & harassment** — targets people or groups with hate speech, slurs or harassment based on protected characteristics.
7. **Illegal activity** — facilitates any illegal act, including fraud, malware distribution or doxxing.
8. **Disinformation** — creates media intended to deceive viewers about real events or statements by real people.

## Uploaded Media

You must own or have permission to use any photo, video or audio you upload. Do not upload media featuring people who have not consented to its use in AI-generated content.

## Moderation & Enforcement

We may moderate, suspend or remove content and accounts that violate this policy. When production generation is enabled, uploaded media and prompts may pass through automated moderation before processing.

## Reporting

To report abusive content, contact us at support@airumpelstiltskin.pro with a link and a short description of the issue.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
