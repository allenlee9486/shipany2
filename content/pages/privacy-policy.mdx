## Introduction

AI Rumpelstiltskin is an independent creative project that turns your photos into short AI-generated dance videos inspired by a viral internet format. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

## Information We Collect

1. **Account information** — your name and email address, provided when you create an account or sign in with Google. If you use Google sign-in, we receive basic profile details (name, email address, profile picture) from Google.
2. **Photos, prompts and generated content** — the photos you upload for character replacement, any scene description you write, and the videos generated for you, together with related task records.
3. **Payment information** — payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your card number. We receive limited details such as the payment status, amount, and the email address used at checkout.
4. **Usage and device data** — the pages you visit, features you use, and general device and browser information.
5. **Cookies** — strictly necessary cookies (for example, to keep you signed in and to remember your language preference) and analytics cookies (Google Analytics).

## How We Use Your Information

- generate your videos and deliver them to your account;
- operate the credit system, orders and subscriptions;
- provide customer support and respond to your requests;
- prevent abuse, fraud and violations of our Acceptable Use Policy;
- understand how the service is used so we can improve it.

We do not sell your personal information.

## AI Processing and Service Providers

To produce a video, the photos you upload and your scene description are transmitted to our AI generation provider (fal.ai and the video model it hosts) so that the video can be generated. Your uploaded photos and the resulting videos are stored in our object storage (Cloudflare R2). We also rely on the following providers to operate the service: Vercel (website hosting), Neon (database), Stripe (payment processing), Google (sign-in and analytics). Each provider processes data only as needed to deliver its service, under its own privacy terms.

## Storage, Retention and Deletion

Your account data is retained while your account is active. Uploaded photos and generated videos are kept so that you can access and download them. You can ask us to delete your uploaded photos, videos or your entire account at any time by emailing us — we will act on such requests within a reasonable period. Some records (for example, payment and tax records) may be kept longer where required by law.

## Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit, access controls, and reputable infrastructure providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children

The service is not directed to children. You must be at least 13 years old (or the minimum age of digital consent in your jurisdiction) to use it. Uploading media involving minors in any inappropriate context is strictly prohibited. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below. You can also control cookies through your browser settings, and opt out of Google Analytics using Google's opt-out tools.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the service after changes means you accept the updated policy.
