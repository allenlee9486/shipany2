The first public version of AI Rumpelstiltskin is live. Upload one or two photos, describe a scene if you like, and get a 9-second dance video in the viral Rumpelstiltskin format — with the original dance audio included.

### What you can do

- **Photo-to-video generation** — the people in your photos take the place of the characters in the reference template, keeping the same dance, camera work and timing.
- **Two characters** — start with the little man, optionally add the princess, and describe the outfit, scene or finishing move.
- **Three formats** — portrait for TikTok and Reels, square for feeds, or landscape to follow the original template's shots exactly.

### Credits and pricing

- 1 video costs **100 credits**. Unused credits never expire, and failed generations are refunded automatically.
- One-time packs start at **$9.99**, or subscribe monthly for a steady supply of credits.

### Good to know

- Generation usually takes **5–15 minutes**. Keep the page open while it runs — and if you do close it, your video is still generated and saved to your account.
- Use photos you have the rights to, with consent from everyone depicted. See our [Acceptable Use Policy](/acceptable-use) for the full rules.
